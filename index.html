How It Works

From raw feedback to clear action in minutes

Connect your tools, and ChurnIntercept does the rest — clustering, scoring, predicting, and delivering intelligence to every team.

1

Connect your sources

Plug in Zendesk, Intercom, Salesforce, Jira, App Store reviews, and more. Or upload a CSV. ChurnIntercept ingests everything.

2

AI clusters & scores

Our AI groups similar feedback, identifies root causes, predicts churn risk per account, and quantifies revenue impact for every issue.

3

Intelligence delivered

Each team gets their view: PMs get roadmap priorities, CS gets churn alerts, Engineering gets bug clusters, Execs get health scores.

4

Ship & track impact

Push tickets to Jira or Linear, send alerts to Slack or Teams, and track week-over-week whether your fixes are moving the needle.
