The current early-access workflow focuses on one job: helping teams organise and prioritise feedback. Planned connectors are labeled as planned until they are genuinely available.

  1. 01

    Bring feedback together

    Start with pasted text or CSV in the browser demo. Zendesk, Intercom, HubSpot, and Salesforce connectors are on the early-access roadmap.

  2. 02

    Review grouped themes

    See which messages were grouped together and why. Teams can challenge the grouping instead of trusting an unexplained score.

  3. 03

    Choose the next action

    Turn a recurring theme into a product discovery question, support intervention, bug investigation, or roadmap candidate.
