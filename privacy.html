Privacy Notice

Your data, in plain language.

This notice explained the limited personal data used by the ChurnIntercept early-access website. It did not describe a production customer-data platform, because that service was not yet offered through this site.

Effective date: July 24, 2026 · Contact: privacy@churnintercept.co.uk

Information collected

The early-access form collected the name, work email, company, plan interest, optional workflow notes, referral source, submission time, and recorded consent supplied by the person submitting it.

Why it was used

The information was used to review early-access requests, respond to the requester, understand product interest, prevent abuse, and maintain a record of the request. It was not used for advertising profiles or sold to third parties.

Legal basis

Contact about an early-access request was based on the requester’s consent. Basic security and abuse-prevention processing was based on legitimate interests in operating a safe website. Consent could be withdrawn at any time.

Service provider

Form submissions were processed through Netlify Forms as the website’s form-handling provider. ChurnIntercept did not intentionally send early-access data to additional form processors, advertising networks, or analytics services.

Interactive demo

The demo processed text locally in the browser. Demo text was not uploaded, stored, or attached to an early-access request. Refreshing or closing the tab cleared the analysed result.

Cookies and analytics

The website did not set non-essential analytics or advertising cookies. Essential infrastructure logs could still be created by the hosting provider for security and delivery purposes.

Retention

Early-access requests were retained only while they remained relevant to evaluating and contacting potential participants, unless a longer period was required to resolve a legal or security issue. Requests to delete information could be sent to the privacy contact above.

Rights

Depending on location, a person could request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or withdrawal of consent. A complaint could also be made to the relevant data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner’s Office where applicable.

International processing

Hosting and form providers could process information in locations covered by their service terms and safeguards. The website did not promise a specific data-residency region for early-access requests.

Changes

This notice could be updated as the product and its data processing changed. Material changes would be reflected by a new effective date.
