ChurnIntercept (churnintercept.co.uk) is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under registration ZC278584 in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: email you use to subscribe to £7.99/mo plan
  • Chat data: text you send to our AI bots (tech questions, Vinted listing URLs, device issues)
  • Images you upload for scam checking (Vinted photos, iPhone listings, router photos)
  • Payment data: processed by Stripe — we do NOT store card numbers
  • Usage data: pages visited, device type

3. Why we collect it

To provide AI tech support at 2/3am, to analyse Vinted/eBay listings for scam risk (0% / 50% / 100% fraud rating), to detect AI vs real influencers (Fanvue / OF), and to improve our models.

4. How long we keep it

Chat and uploaded images: 90 days unless you ask us to delete sooner. Account and billing: 6 years for HMRC. You can request deletion anytime via support@churnintercept.co.uk

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, and data portability. Contact us at support@churnintercept.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the ICO: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ quoting our registration ZC278584.

6. Sharing

We share data only with: Stripe (payments), OpenAI / AI providers (to analyse your tech question or image — anonymised), and hosting providers (GitHub Pages / Vercel). We do NOT sell data.

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for login and Stripe checkout. No tracking cookies for ads.
